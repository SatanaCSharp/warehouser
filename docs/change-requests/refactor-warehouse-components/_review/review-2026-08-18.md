**CHANGES REQUESTED.**

No stage-1 finding may ship unresolved. S1 and S2 are the blocking pair: they are live `docs/system`
text, Accepted by this very change, stating a rule that its own decision procedure refutes — a
contributor applying it as written reaches the opposite of what `spec.md` §3 declares an

reorganization, the state audit and the neighbour fences all verify cleanly against
`baseline_revision`, with zero assertion drift across 39 cases and 135 expectations. What is
outstanding is the justification text, four test assertions, and the honesty of the records.

CR-RG-06 remains **blocked, not satisfied** — no container runtime is available, and per `spec.md`
an unrunnable suite is not evidence of identical failure. The CR-RG-07 bound (no file under
`apps/server/` modified) is independently confirmed and bounds the risk, but does not discharge the
criterion.
