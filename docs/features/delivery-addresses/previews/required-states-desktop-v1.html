Delivery addresses — required states

Loading, empty, authorization, refusal and success states for customers, delivery addresses, delivery mode and per-line endings. Each tile renders inside the shells above; none is a screen of its own.

LOADING · CUSTOMERS

Search customers or addresses

Announced as “Loading customers”. Nothing is requested for a dataset the actor may not read, so a member without CUSTOMERS:WATCH never sees this state — the destination does not exist for them.

EMPTY · NO CUSTOMERS YET · AC-01

No customers recorded yet

Record a customer once and every order you take for them names the record instead of a spelling. A customer belongs to this warehouse alone — the same company in another warehouse is a separate record with its own addresses.

NO CUSTOMERS:WATCH · AC-09

  • Dashboard
  • Demand
  • Purchase drafts
  • Items
  • Access

The Customers entry is absent — not disabled, not empty — and the destination is unreachable. A denial shows no customer name, address, quantity or item, and presents no count from which the member could infer that any customer exists.

CUSTOMER IDENTITY WITHHELD · AC-09a

Customer withheld Unfulfilled · 800 outstanding · needed by 2 Sep 2026
Customer withheld Unfulfilled · 440 outstanding · needed by 9 Sep 2026

A member holding PURCHASE_DRAFTS:WATCH and CUSTOMER_ORDERS:WATCH but not CUSTOMERS:WATCH reads the draft, the demand and the drift with every customer name withheld — a recorded customer's and a typed one's alike — together with the addresses and access notes. Quantities, dates and items stay visible, because those are what their own permissions admit.

ARCHIVED WAREHOUSE · READ ONLY · AC-23

Archived warehouse

This warehouse is archived

Its customers, delivery addresses and purchase drafts read exactly as they did before archiving. Recording a customer, redirecting an order and recording a line's ending are refused, because an archived warehouse authorizes no operation that changes what it holds.

Mutating controls stay visible and disabled with the reason exposed, rather than disappearing — the member should see that the capability exists and why it is closed.

ANOTHER WAREHOUSE'S CUSTOMER · AC-12

No customer of this warehouse has that name

Demand names a customer of the warehouse you are acting in. Record the customer here if you serve them from this warehouse too.

The refusal never discloses that the customer or the address exists in another warehouse, even when the member holds a membership and the matching permission there. A customer and its addresses never cross the warehouse boundary.

LAST ACTIVE ADDRESS · AC-07

A customer always keeps at least one active delivery address

Lagerplatz 9, 24937 Flensburg is the only active address Rheinmetall Verpackung has. Add the replacement address first, then deactivate this one.

The refusal holds whether or not the customer is still waiting for goods, and it changes nothing. The order the member must follow — add, then deactivate — is stated rather than left to be discovered.

MAIN ADDRESS MOVED AUTOMATICALLY · AC-06b

Hafenstraße 14, 20457 Hamburg is now inactive

It was the main address, so Werftweg 2, 27568 Bremerhaven is now the main delivery address for Nordwind Logistik GmbH.

Deactivating the main address never leaves a customer without one. The system picks a remaining active address and names it — it is never left for the member to discover on the next order.

INACTIVE ADDRESS NOT OFFERED · AC-06a

An inactive address is absent from the picker rather than shown and disabled, with a line saying so — the same rule an inactive item follows. Every order and every frozen line that already names it keeps reading and counting exactly as before.

DIRECT LINE, DEMAND ELSEWHERE · AC-15 / AC-15a

This line ships to one address, and that order goes to another

Line 2 ships direct to Hafenstraße 14, 20457 Hamburg. The order you are linking goes to Werftweg 2, 27568 Bremerhaven. A directly-shipped line serves only the demand going to the address it ships to.

The same check runs whenever the link is made, whenever the line is revised, and again when the draft is moved to Ready for ordering — every disagreeing link is named and none is withdrawn, because which one to drop is the member's decision.

YOUR OWN ADDRESS ON A DIRECT LINE · AC-14

The error is bound to the field that holds the wrong value, and it names the rule rather than the request. Switching the line back to Via warehouse resolves it without retyping anything.

READY REFUSED · NO WAREHOUSE ADDRESS · AC-16a

This warehouse has no delivery address yet

Line 1 comes to your warehouse, and it cannot be frozen before there is an address for the supplier to deliver to. Someone holding WAREHOUSES:ADDRESS_UPDATE records it on the workspace's Warehouses tab.

Nothing changes and the draft stays exactly as it was. Adding and revising lines keeps working — only the move to Ready for ordering is refused, and the capability that unblocks it is named.

ENDING ALREADY RECORDED · AC-20a

This line's ending is already recorded

Line 1 was recorded as arrived on 26 August at 09:14 by Yurii Horchuk. A line's ending is recorded once, and nothing further is assigned to any customer order.

Recording an ending twice would reduce what a customer is waiting for a second time from goods that came once. The refusal names when and by whom, so the member can tell a duplicate submission from a colleague's work.

WRONG ENDING FOR THIS LINE · AC-20

Line 2's goods went straight to the customer

You are recording an arrival at your dock, and that line never reaches your building. Record a delivery to the customer instead — arrivals belong to lines travelling via warehouse.

Each of the two endings is refused against the other delivery mode, and the refusal says which of the two ways that line's goods actually travelled rather than only that the request was rejected.
