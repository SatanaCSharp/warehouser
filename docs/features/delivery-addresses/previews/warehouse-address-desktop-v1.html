Delivery address

The address a supplier is told for every line coming to this warehouse. A warehouse has exactly one — you correct it in place and it is never deactivated.

A draft cannot be made ready before this is recorded

Any purchase draft holding a line that comes to this warehouse needs an address for the supplier to deliver to. Adding and revising lines keeps working; only the move to Ready for ordering is refused until this is filled in.

People with access

Giving access places a person in this warehouse with a role defined there. What they may do inside it is decided by that role, not by the workspace — manage those roles on the warehouse's Access page.
